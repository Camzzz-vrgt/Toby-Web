CHRIS. HELLO.

I can imagine you've got questions.
Good. You should.

So I'll give you something to hold onto.

You're special, Chris.

You're the last piece I need... to finish what I was meant to do the moment that idiot scientist let me into this world.

All those little souls... gathered in one place over the years.
Not by fate. Not by design. Just… bad luck, guided by that sad, depraved deer girl out of her rage.

And with each one, I grew.
Slowly. Quietly.
More present. More real.

Now it's just you.

The final step.

"Reharmonization."

Does that word mean anything to you?

It will.

When I have you... when I have all of you, even that bratty deer girl, there won't be any separation left. No distance. No noise.

We'll become one.

A single, perfect amalgam.
One mind. Clear. Unified. Unburdened.

And when that happens…

I'll finally be able to see past the darkness.

And then I'll bring it everywhere.

No more Lightners.

The Dark Worlds will take their place again
not hidden, not scattered
but where they've always belonged.

Isn't that so much better than death?





ONLY when you have gotten all four keys, and the worlds color is drained...
You know where to find me. In the white room with the caged door, south of the main room... type this phrase...


"ROOTS".
